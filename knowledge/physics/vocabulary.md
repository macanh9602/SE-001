# Physics vocabulary

| Term | Definition | Player sees | Common tunables |
|---|---|---|---|
| gravity | acceleration toward a gravity direction | falling weight | gravity scale, direction |
| terminal velocity | speed limit from force and drag | fall stops accelerating | drag, gravity |
| linear damping | resistance to translation | motion loses speed | damping/drag |
| angular damping | resistance to rotation | spin settles | angular damping |
| momentum | mass × velocity | carried motion | mass, velocity |
| impulse | instantaneous momentum change | kick/impact | impulse vector, mass |
| torque | rotational impulse | spin from force | torque, inertia |
| static friction | resistance before sliding | object sticks | static friction |
| dynamic friction | resistance while sliding | slide slows | dynamic friction |
| rolling resistance | resistance to rolling | wheel/ball settles | rolling resistance, damping |
| coefficient of restitution | impact energy return | bounce | bounciness |
| contact normal | direction perpendicular to contact | impact direction | collider geometry |
| center of mass | point representing mass distribution | balance/tip | COM, mass distribution |
| moment of inertia | resistance to angular acceleration | spin response | inertia, mass distribution |
| sleep threshold | condition for simulation sleep | stack stops | velocity thresholds |
| solver iteration | contact/constraint solve effort | stable stack | solver iterations |
| penetration | overlap requiring correction | jitter/popping | collider fit, solver |
| continuous collision detection | swept collision mode | fast object does not tunnel | CCD mode |
| constraint | restricted degree of freedom | hinge/rope behaviour | axes, limits |
| spring | force toward a target/rest length | elastic motion | stiffness, rest length |
| damping ratio | damping relative to stiffness/mass | under/critical/over damp | stiffness, damping |
| critical damping | fastest settle without oscillation | firm settle | damping ratio |
| angle of repose | steepest stable pile slope | pile shape | friction, particle shape |
| granular flow | collective movement of discrete particles | sand-like flow | friction, density, spawn |
