# Physics behaviour catalogue

- Free motion: fall, launch, drift, drag.
- Contact: impact, bounce, penetration correction.
- Tangential contact: slide, friction, roll.
- Rotation: torque, spin, angular settle.
- Rest/settle: sleep, stable duration, authored settle.
- Constraints: joint, spring, rope, limit.
- Force field: attraction, repulsion, suction, wind.
- Aggregate/granular: piles, flow, many-body contacts.
- Fluid-like fake: authored fields and visual approximation.
- Hybrid authored: deterministic command/result with short physical presentation.

Decompose a behaviour as Release → free motion → first impact → bounce/slide/roll/tumble → secondary contacts → energy decay → settle/sleep.
